**Test budget, where all three of us disagree:** The Believer and Skeptic propose $300–500. At a likely $50–78 CAC, $500 buys only **6–10 purchases**. That is too few to prove profit, but it is enough to kill the idea. I am changing my condition to a **two-stage gate**:
- **Stage 1 ($500):** Stop if there are fewer than 10 purchases (CAC > $50), or if CTR is below 1% (the Skeptic's threshold).
- **Stage 2 ($1,000, only if Stage 1 passes):** My original condition. That is at least 40 purchases at a blended CAC of $20 or less and front-end ROAS of 1.2 or more.

This caps the worst-case loss at about **$500 in ads + ~$100 in tools**, down from up to $3,000.

**Revised break-even / verdict**
- Break-even conversion rate needed (CPC $1.24): **5.9%** with the funnel, and **8.1–8.9%** for a single book (depending on Payhip or Gumroad fees). The likely conversion rate is **1.6–2.5%**, so the gap is **2.4–3.7x** for the funnel and about 3–5.5x for a single book. That gap is wider than in my Round 1.
- Cash to break-even if it works: still **$2,000–5,000, likely about $3,000, over 2–4 months**. With the stage gate, cash at risk before the first kill decision is **about $600**.
- **Verdict: still Unattractive as a single e-book, and Marginal-to-Unattractive as a funnel.** The best-sourced conversion data moved my likely CAC up, from about $50 to $50–78. None of the Believer's points closes a gap of 2.4x or more. The only thing that improved is the downside, which is now capped by the staged test. It is worth a $500 experiment and not worth more until Stage 1 passes.
