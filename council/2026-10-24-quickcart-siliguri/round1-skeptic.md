## Skeptic — Round 1
**Biggest single reason this fails:** There is no evidence yet that any customer wants this, and the unit economics (₹7 margin per order) leave no room for the costs the brief leaves out.

**Risks**
1. No demand evidence on the customer side. The brief gives no customer, no problem, and no customer conversations. The only evidence is 5 vendor chats, 3 "interested". — [Fact] Customer and Problem are "Not provided" (from the brief). Vendor interest is not the same as buyers ordering. — severity 5
   Cheapest test (≤ 2 weeks): Take orders by WhatsApp from about 30 households near 2-3 vendors, with no app, delivering by hand or on your own bike. Kill if fewer than about 10 people place a second order within 2 weeks. Save if repeat ordering reaches roughly 1 order per household per week.

2. Thin unit margin and no covered fixed costs. ₹25 fee minus ₹18 cost leaves ₹7 per delivery. — [Fact] Figures from the brief. — [Assumption] The ₹18 may exclude rider idle time, cash shortfalls, phone and data costs, packaging, refunds, and your own time; a real figure usually runs higher. Even at ₹7 clean margin, ₹40,000 needs about 5,700 deliveries just to be repaid (40,000 / 7, my arithmetic). — severity 4
   Cheapest test: Run 20-30 real deliveries and log all costs, including empty rider time between orders. Kill if the true cost is ₹25 or more. Save if it is ₹18 or less at 3 or more orders per rider-hour.

3. Competition from quick-commerce apps and from the vendors themselves. — [Assumption] Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart, BigBasket and JioMart may already serve Siliguri. The search only turned up an indirect signal (a job-board page saying Zepto hires delivery staff in Siliguri), so I could not confirm any app's coverage. Many local grocers also already deliver by phone and WhatsApp. Vendors may take your customer list and deliver themselves. — severity 4
   Cheapest test: Enter pincodes 734001 and 734003 in each app and note which serve them, their delivery fee and their time. Ask 5 vendors: "Do you already deliver, and what do you charge?" Kill if the apps cover your target areas with free or cheap delivery AND vendors already deliver for free. Save if gaps exist, such as outer wards, small-basket orders, or evening slots.

4. Cash on delivery creates leakage and float risk. Riders carry cash. You must settle with vendors, probably before you collect it. — [Assumption] A ₹40,000 buffer can be tied up or lost to a few large disputes or theft. There is no sourced evidence on COD loss rates in this market. — severity 3
   Cheapest test: Write out the settlement flow on paper (who pays the vendor, when, how). Do it daily with UPI payouts to vendors. Cap the order value at about ₹500 in the pilot. Kill if you cannot settle without pre-funding more than about ₹5,000 a day.

5. Vendor commitment is weak and the range is narrow. 3 of 5 were "interested", not signed. 10 vendors may not stock a full basket, so orders get split or substituted. — [Fact] Counts from the brief. — [Assumption] "Interested" will not turn into stock accuracy or fast packing. — severity 3
   Cheapest test: Get 3 vendors to agree in writing (even by WhatsApp) to a 2-week pilot with defined packing time and out-of-stock rules. Kill if fewer than 3 will commit. Save if 3 commit and the out-of-stock rate stays under 15%.

Legal and compliance: I did not research this. [Assumption] You may need GST registration and FSSAI-related rules where you handle food, and rider employment rules. Check this before scaling, not before the pilot.

**What would change my mind:** 10 or more repeat WhatsApp customers who pay the ₹25 fee, a measured all-in delivery cost at or below ₹18, and evidence that apps and vendors do not already cover the same areas for free. With that, a small paid pilot with no app build is reasonable. Without it, do not spend the ₹40,000.

Sources: [Zepto delivery jobs in Siliguri (job board, indirect signal)](https://www.jobhai.com/zepto-delivery-jobs-in-siliguri-ccgcmp). Opening pages was not working, so I could not verify any app's Siliguri coverage and tagged it [Assumption].
